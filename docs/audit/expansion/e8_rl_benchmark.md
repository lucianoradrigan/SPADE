## Reading

- **Buck: RL loses on steady-state tracking.** The PI reaches 0.19 V median RMSE in the second half of every condition; DQN 0.49 V and DDPG 0.9–4.6 V depending on the seed.
- **Reward and tracking disagree.** DQN (−3.10) and DDPG seed 1 (−4.66) score a *better* mean episode reward than the PI (−5.03), because the reward covers the whole episode (start-up included) and adds the action-rate term, while the RMSE column measures the second half only; which part of the episode makes the difference was not decomposed. A policy that wins on reward is not thereby a better regulator; both numbers are reported.
- **Seed sensitivity is large** for DDPG on the buck (median RMSE 0.93–4.62 V over seeds 0–2 with the same budget).
- **VSC: RL is far from the DPC networks** (median RMSE 21.5 V against 13.2 V for DPC v3 and 2.8 V for DPC v4) after 100 episodes; the DPC networks were trained on the identified model with a differentiable predictive-control loss, which a model-free agent with this budget does not match.
- Training budget: 100 episodes per run (1000 steps each), seed per run as listed, 2 TF threads per process. Longer training was not tried.
