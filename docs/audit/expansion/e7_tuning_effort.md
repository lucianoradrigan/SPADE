## Reading (written after the run)

1. **High bandwidth fails under sensor noise.**
   - Every candidate at 142–160 rad/s enters a noise-driven limit cycle on the 5 rad/s step: 51.7 % overshoot, a 53 V RMS change of the voltage command per step (the command swings between its limits), and no settling inside the run.
   - This holds for the first E7 candidate and for the PSO and Bayesian picks on J_effort.
   - With 0.6 rad/s sensor noise, the baseline itself already uses 2.2 V of effort, against 0.15 V noise-free.
2. **The effort term did not stop PSO and BO from going to high bandwidth.**
   - The tuning grid is noise-free, so on its two conditions the effort term changes little with the gains.
   - Making the effort term bite would need sensor noise **in the tuning grid** too.
   - That change was not made after seeing these results: it is a proposal, and it needs its own run.
3. **NSGA-II** kept the bandwidth lower (118 rad/s, b = 0.33).
   - Its overshoot is close to C2's: 3.2 % on the 5 rad/s step and 6.9 % on the drift step.
   - Its effort is 1.4× the baseline's on those steps.
   - On the saturating step it shows the same 53 V chatter.
4. **C2 (setpoint weight only, bandwidth unchanged) is the better route for S-04.** With b = 0.5:
   - overshoot is 14.8 % → 2.4 % on the 5 rad/s step, and 19.9 % → 8.5 % on the drift step;
   - the effort is unchanged (2.2 V);
   - the peak current is lower (114 A and 102 A, against 155 A and 120 A).
   - The load dip with noise is larger (6.3 vs 4.6 rad/s), while noise-free it is 4.32 vs 4.17. Setpoint weighting does not change the disturbance path in the linear model, so this difference is measured but not explained here.
5. **The 97 A constraint is broken by every controller on the evaluation grid, the baseline included.**
   - The saturating 30 rad/s step and the 15 Nm load at 200 rad/s need more than the rated current by design: the PI clips the current reference at 0.8 × 210 A = 168 A.
   - The constraint is informative only on the non-saturating steps, and there C2 b = 0.5 has the lowest peaks.
6. **Settling time with noise is not informative.** The 2 % band of a 2–5 rad/s step (0.04–0.1 rad/s) is narrower than the speed jitter, so most noisy settling times sit at the end of the window (≈ 300 ms) or never enter the band (—).
