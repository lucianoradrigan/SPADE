## Reading

- The objective has no control-effort, current-margin or noise term, so a faster speed loop always reduces the load dip and the optimisers push the bandwidth towards its upper bound. A candidate at the bound is a property of this objective, not evidence that the faster loop is safe on a real drive.
- The evaluation grid includes plant drift and a current-saturating step; it is the generalisation estimate, the tuning-grid J is not.
