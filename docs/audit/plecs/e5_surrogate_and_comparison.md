## Reading

- **ZOH grey box: zero error is expected here, not a result about real circuits.** The data come from the same averaged model the grey box assumes, without noise, so the identification is exact. Against a switched PLECS circuit (ripple, dead time, losses) its error would be the model mismatch, which this run cannot measure.
- **MLP: the black box generalises across the held-out conditions but drifts in open loop** (relative v_out errors of a few % to tens of %, worst at the edge of the domain, 60 V / 24 Ω). In closed loop the PI corrects most of it; the max |v_out| deviations are a fraction of a volt inside the domain and grow at the 1.5 Ω step outside it.
- The default family was chosen on the validation conditions only; the test rows are reported, not used.
