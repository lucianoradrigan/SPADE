## SPADE v0.2.0

**All results are simulated.** Release notes: `docs/RELEASE_NOTES_v0.2.0.md`.

### What is in it (since `main` at `df1950f`)

- **Phase 0 simulator options:** DC load torque, supply, limits and nominal values; PMSM motor parameters; the plant catalog (`describe_plant`); the `dc_pmg132_v1` plant.
- **The v2 DC plants** (`dc_perm_ex_v2`, `dc_pmg132_v2`, converter `dcm_exact`) are the default of the GUI, the assistant and the DC dataset scripts. They correct S-21. The v1 plants stay bit for bit as the GEM-faithful mode.
- **AI-N27:** the PC and RPi5 dc_motor classifiers were retrained on the corrected converter and promoted (2026-11-04).
- **Assistant** (experimental / internal demo): local LLM through Ollama. Its pre-registered benchmarks F1.11–F1.13 were not met (AI-N30). The avatar is under its own license, outside MIT.
- **AI-N26:** every run records its sampling rate, and the C2000 timing was re-read post hoc at 10 kHz.
- **Documentation:** `docs/PLATFORM_STATUS.md`, `docs/VALIDATION_SUMMARY.md` §14, README.
