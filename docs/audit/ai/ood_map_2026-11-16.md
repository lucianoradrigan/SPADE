## Reading (descriptive; burned data, no decision may follow from it)

- **Are the declared envelopes right?** Yes, on this map. Inside each model's envelope the share of confidently wrong windows is 0.000 for the three shipped models and at most 0.057 (PC B4 candidate at 40 rad/s, its lower edge); no healthy window is confidently misread inside any envelope.
- **Outside them the risk is real and grows with the distance:** above the grid, errors appear from about 280 rad/s (PC 0.09–0.63 confident-wrong; RPi5 shipped up to 0.63, and from 320 rad/s every healthy window is misread); below it, the shipped PC is confidently wrong at 40 (0.40) and 20 rad/s (0.57, healthy windows all misread): AI-N29 and AI-N40 again.
- **The envelopes are conservative:** 240–260 rad/s are clean for every model on this map. Widening an envelope would need a pre-registered evaluation on new data (these are burned).
- **ESP32 (shipped):** clean at 60 rad/s and 40 rad/s (0.06) on these noise-free samples, while E4 (50/70 rad/s, with noise and plant drift) found outer_race recall 0.28 (AI-N43). The map uses one noise-free run per cell, so it is coarser than E4: no contradiction is claimed.
- One run per (speed, load, class), noise-free, nominal plant: a map of where to look, not an accuracy estimate.
